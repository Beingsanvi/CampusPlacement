# University Placement Policy

## 1. Eligibility Criteria

### General Requirements
- Minimum CGPA: 7.0 (on a 10-point scale)
- No active academic backlogs at the time of placement
- Good academic standing with no disciplinary actions
- Must be enrolled in a full-time degree program

### Registration Requirements
- Students must register for placements through the Training & Placement Cell
- Registration deadline: Usually 2 weeks before the first company visit
- Complete registration form with all required documents
- Attend mandatory orientation session

## 2. Student Responsibilities

### Code of Conduct
- Maintain professional behavior during all placement activities
- Attend all scheduled interviews and assessments
- Provide accurate information in applications
- Respect company representatives and placement staff

### Documentation
- Keep all academic certificates updated
- Maintain a valid photo ID
- Carry multiple copies of resume and certificates
- Have passport-size photographs ready

## 3. Placement Offer Rules

### Offer Acceptance
- Students must respond to offers within the specified deadline
- Once an offer is accepted, it cannot be declined without valid reason
- Accepting an offer and later declining may result in placement ban

### Multiple Offers
- Students may receive offers from multiple companies
- First valid offer must be accepted unless otherwise specified
- Declining subsequent offers after acceptance requires written permission

## 4. General Guidelines

### Attendance
- 100% attendance is mandatory for placement activities
- Medical emergencies must be reported immediately with valid documentation
- Unauthorized absence may result in placement suspension

### Communication
- Check placement portal regularly for updates
- Respond to all official communications within 24 hours
- Report any issues to the placement coordinator immediately

## 5. Grievance Redressal

### Process
- Submit grievances in writing to the Placement Cell
- Response within 5 working days
- Appeal process available for unresolved issues

*This policy is subject to change. Students will be notified of any updates.*
