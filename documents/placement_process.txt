# Campus Placement Process

## Overview

The campus placement process follows a structured approach to connect students with potential employers. This document outlines the complete placement workflow.

## 1. Registration Phase

### Timeline
- Registration opens: 4 weeks before first company visit
- Registration deadline: 2 weeks before company visit
- Late registration: Not allowed

### Steps
1. **Online Registration**
   - Fill placement registration form
   - Upload required documents
   - Pay registration fee (if applicable)
   - Submit resume

2. **Document Verification**
   - Academic certificates verified
   - Resume reviewed
   - Eligibility confirmed

3. **Orientation Session**
   - Placement process overview
   - Code of conduct briefing
   - Q&A session

## 2. Pre-Placement Activities

### Company Announcements
- Company details shared via placement portal
- Job description and requirements posted
- Eligibility criteria specified
- Application deadline announced

### Student Preparation
- Review company requirements
- Update resume if needed
- Prepare for specific assessment
- Attend pre-placement talks (if scheduled)

## 3. Eligibility Check

### Verification Process
- CGPA verification
- Backlog status check
- Branch eligibility confirmation
- Attendance record review

### Common Disqualifications
- CGPA below minimum requirement
- Active backlogs
- Disciplinary issues
- Incomplete registration

## 4. Assessment Phase

### Types of Assessments

#### Online Tests
- **Aptitude Test**: 45-60 minutes
  - Logical reasoning
  - Quantitative aptitude
  - Verbal ability

- **Coding Test**: 60-90 minutes
  - 2-4 coding problems
  - Focus on DSA and problem-solving
  - Language: Python, Java, C++

- **Technical Test**: 30-60 minutes
  - Domain-specific questions
  - Technical concepts
  - Problem-solving scenarios

#### Group Discussion (if applicable)
- Topic discussion (15-20 minutes)
- Team collaboration assessment
- Communication skills evaluation

### Assessment Tips
- Time management is crucial
- Read questions carefully
- Start with easier problems
- Manage stress and stay calm

## 5. Interview Phase

### Technical Interview

#### Round 1 (30-45 minutes)
- Programming concepts
- Data structures and algorithms
- Basic technical questions
- Project discussion

#### Round 2 (30-45 minutes)
- Advanced technical concepts
- System design (for experienced roles)
- Problem-solving approach
- Technical depth assessment

### Managerial Interview (if applicable)
- Leadership qualities
- Problem-solving approach
- Team collaboration
- Decision-making skills

### HR Interview (20-30 minutes)
- Behavioral questions
- Career goals
- Company fit assessment
- Salary negotiation

## 6. Selection Process

### Evaluation Criteria
- Assessment scores
- Interview performance
- Technical skills
- Communication skills
- Cultural fit

### Selection Timeline
- Results typically announced within 1-2 weeks
- Offer letters shared via email
- Acceptance deadline specified

## 7. Offer & Acceptance

### Offer Details
- Job role and designation
- Salary package (CTC)
- Joining date
- Location
- Terms and conditions

### Acceptance Process
1. Review offer details carefully
2. Accept/decline within deadline
3. Sign offer letter if accepting
4. Notify placement cell

### Post-Acceptance
- Mandatory to join on specified date
- Cannot decline without valid reason
- Violation may result in placement ban

## 8. Post-Placement

### For Selected Students
- Receive onboarding information
- Complete pre-joining formalities
- Join company on specified date

### For Unselected Students
- Continue preparing for next opportunities
- Attend next company drives
- Seek feedback if available

## 9. Important Contacts

### Placement Cell
- Placement Coordinator
- Training & Placement Officer
- Department Placement Representatives

### Communication Channels
- Placement Portal
- Email notifications
- WhatsApp groups (if applicable)
- Notice board announcements
