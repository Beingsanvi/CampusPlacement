# Placement Preparation Guidelines

## 1. Registration Preparation

### Documents Required
- Updated Resume (2-3 copies)
- All academic certificates (10th, 12th, Degree)
- Photo ID proof
- Passport-size photographs (5-6 copies)
- Internship certificates (if any)
- Project certificates (if any)
- Offer letter (for placement acceptance)

### Resume Tips
- Keep resume to 1-2 pages
- Highlight relevant skills and projects
- Include technical skills section
- Mention internships and certifications
- Use professional format

## 2. Assessment Preparation

### Aptitude Test Preparation
- **Logical Reasoning**: Practice puzzles, series, coding-decoding
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Focus on arithmetic, algebra, geometry
- **Verbal Ability**: Reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary
- **Data Interpretation**: Charts, graphs, tables analysis

### Recommended Resources
- IndiaBix for aptitude practice
- GeeksforGeeks for coding
- LeetCode for programming challenges
- HackerRank for skill practice

## 3. Technical Interview Preparation

### Core Topics
- **Data Structures**: Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Hash Tables
- **Algorithms**: Sorting, Searching, Dynamic Programming, Greedy
- **Programming**: OOPs concepts, Language-specific features
- **Databases**: SQL basics, Normalization, Indexing
- **Operating Systems**: Processes, Threads, Memory Management
- **Networking**: OSI Model, TCP/IP, HTTP basics

### System Design (for advanced roles)
- Scalability concepts
- Load balancing
- Caching mechanisms
- Database design

## 4. HR Interview Preparation

### Common Questions
- Tell me about yourself
- Why this company?
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Career goals
- Team experience
- Conflict resolution

### Tips
- Research the company thoroughly
- Prepare STAR method examples
- Practice mock interviews
- Maintain professional body language

## 5. Company-Specific Preparation

### Research Each Company
- Company history and mission
- Products/services
- Recent news and developments
- Work culture
- Salary and benefits

### Assessment Patterns
- Some companies focus on coding
- Others emphasize aptitude
- Some have group discussions
- Technical depth varies by role

## 6. Daily Practice Schedule

### Recommended Daily Routine
- **Morning**: 1 hour aptitude practice
- **Afternoon**: 2 hours coding practice
- **Evening**: 1 hour technical concepts
- **Night**: 30 minutes company research

### Weekly Goals
- Solve 5-10 coding problems
- Complete 1-2 aptitude tests
- Study 1-2 technical topics
- Mock interview practice

## 7. Interview Day Tips

### Before Interview
- Get proper sleep
- Dress professionally
- Arrive 15-20 minutes early
- Carry all required documents
- Stay calm and confident

### During Interview
- Listen carefully to questions
- Think before answering
- Be honest about knowledge gaps
- Show enthusiasm and interest
- Ask relevant questions

### After Interview
- Send thank-you email
- Follow up if no response
- Continue preparing for next opportunity

---

*Preparation is key to success. Start early and practice consistently.*
